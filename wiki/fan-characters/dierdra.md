## History

### Before TDay

Prior to [TDay](tday) Dierdra was a lead developer at AHI Syntech. AHI Syntech was originally a military contractor which specialized in the production of combat oriented robots. For all her talent in her craft, Dierdra was often sidelined by the men in the C-suites above her. Many of her projects and drafts were dismissed by the board with often little to no reasoning, leading to her resenting the company and those that drove it. Before her change she was somewhat timid and confrontation avoidant, which was the driving factor behind the situations she often found herself in during high level meetings.

On TDay, she was pushed to her breaking point. After a sudden outburst in reaction to the terrible treatment from the executives, an argument would follow that ended in her being terminated on the spot. Moments later however, while still in the board room the effects of [Apotheosis](apotheosis-serum) would take hold. Karma had never been sweeter.

### After TDay

After undergoing her changes into her new self, her personality would shift. She grew more confident in herself. Freed from the shackles she once knew, she lived her life far more easygoing. She found a passion in food, coffee, and aerobics. However, as the years passed she slowly gravitated back towards her previous career. Her knack for engineering and robotics design had not been lost, and now with the bad taste of her prior experiences long since faded, she would recreate AHI Syntech.

Under the umbrella of [SinCorp](sincorp), the new AHI Syntech would be responsible for the design and manufacturing of a large range of new and novel pleasure devices. She viewed this connection between the two as extremely valuable… as something to exploit.

She would design two exoskeletal harnesses later named ["domesticators."](the-domesticator) The first was the "bitch" model, which through a combination of rapid gene therapy, drugs, and mechanical domination would turn the toy placed inside into a purpose built sleeve designed to be bred.
