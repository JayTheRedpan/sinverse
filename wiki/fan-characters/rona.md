## History

Rona was among the first wave of giantesses created artificially after TDay, marking the beginning of a cultural shift in the society of the new world. Like the other early members of this "second-generation" of giantesses, she brought a fresh perspective of the new world order. To her, everything about the new world is normal; it is the old world that she learned so much about from her elders that was strange and unnatural. A world where girls were supposed to respect toys? To even be subservient to them? Revolting. Toys exist for our pleasure and nothing else.

The exposure to the stories of TDay and of how the world used to be caused Rona to develop a great thankfulness for the way the world is now, simultaneously seeing the elements of giantess culture as normal and at the same time not taking anything for granted. She also developed a deep compassion for her fellow girls, who had been through so much and worked so hard to build this new, perfect world. She wanted to find some way to give back, to contribute, to repay all of the effort that her forebears exerted in creating the society that she now enjoyed for free.

She began training herself in psychology, studying old pre-TDay text books and documents on cognitive-behavioral therapy salvaged from the ruins of the previous civilization, even going on expeditions personally to recover lost texts and hard drives. She interviewed first-gen girls who had been in the field before that world ended, and got mentorship from some of them. She did studies and experiments, and consulted the original Sincorp scientists to see what parts of this old knowledge could still be applied to the new, altered giantess psyche.

Finally, she became the first practicing post-TDay therapist, helping other girls with their personal issues, resolving conflicts between couples and friends, and, most of all, treating the trauma that many of the first generation still carried, both from their TDay experience and from their prior lives.
