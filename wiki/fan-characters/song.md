## Overview

Song is a draft horse, or shire breed and a survivor up till the post-TDay world. Placed into a rather unique situation, more due to chance than talent, his resulting fall and rise has found itself in the background to the events surrounding more desirable toys, and girls alike.

He stood 5'9" and while his transformation increased this to a generous 10'9 his features were otherwise unchanged. In the old world he was simply a modest rather secluded figure, something that perhaps was most responsible for his survival in those first hectic years, and what came after.

## Appearance

Song is a stallion though with some oddity's brought about by a mixed heritage, though he does not peak of it, there was some fur and human mixing in his bloodline, resulting in a Lilith build to the usually bulky build his breed it known for. Soft greyish silver fur the feel of velvet, long ivory locks and feathering decorating curves that from most angles make him appear a female, though quickly corrected once someone see's the front and that at least one part of him definitely inherited the stallion genes, even if it lacks the more broad tip, the girth and sheath are more than familiar to anyone of an equine persuasion.

## Personality

Song is a survivor, through efforts both of his own making and other's he's found himself in a position that allows him to slate his desires and impress upon the world a minor bit of change. Though outwardly cocky, and teasing, confident, these are tools he had learned and do not reflect his true nature, as a rather temped, and gentle soul. While not a genius he is shockingly adaptable and able to seek out opportunities and take risks most would find rather insane.

Some have suggested in fact that he does suffer from some level of divergent personalities, while others have simply noted that his acting has likely simply become so much a part of him that it's hard to tell where one ends and the other beings, even for Song himself.

## History

### Before TDay

Prior to T-Day Song was a simple work from home stallion. And without much in the way of person-to-person interaction even if he did credit himself with some close digital friends. Notably he was attempting to become more outgoing and even had a date…ironically set up for the after what would be come to be known as T-Day.

He did not end up attending, and it is joked that his "date" at the time is still hunting the little "TOY!" that stood her up.

### After TDay

Song survived TDay — details were short, and while open about some notes Song does not like to talk about it. In his words it was rather simple, run, hide, repeat, till you get away, or get caught. What is know is that while being spotted by several Giantess's during those periods, he was never officially caught.

Some rumors suggest that this was in part due to his appearance, as a Giantess's first instinct when seeing an untransformed girl is not aggression, perhaps giving him the moment he would need to escape, however this is unconfirmed.

### As a Toy

This period is only slightly known about, as at some point, Song was spotted in the possession of Hydie's pound. However, little is known about these periods outside of the fact that no official PAW records record him being captured.

His first official notation was being modified at a SIN Corp facility and registered under Hydie, as an official toy for her. During this period, he was upgraded with a standard resiliency package, increased durability and associated effects. A notation was made during the process that he was shockingly obedient for an unaltered toy ironically leading to higher praise of Hydie and a misunderstanding as to the nature of her pound.

### As a Sylph

At some point Song was registered for the Sylph program officially graduating following its completion. The exact process seems to leave Song in a unique case with his empathy mostly intact, while within a body now built and in need of release and power over those below him. Song is the first noted case of a tempered Sylph, though there is no official notation that SIN corp is aware of this deviation.

Due to SIN regulations the exact events are not known, and Song adamantly refuses to discuss how he "passed" the tests. Only Hydie and those closest to him have any idea of the actual events, or why he seems so bothered by them. As a Sylph he is granted freedom to navigate mega cities with consideration to his status. While this isn't true freedom it is more than any Toy could dream of. He is known to apply that influence in order to retrieve toys when possible, returning them to the Pound and via it to other resistance organizations. Those of a smaller stature, of the shrunken persuasion unable to be relocated he keeps in his loft above the pound granted to him by Hydie.

Due to his transformation, his bodily needs and drives are similar to those of any effected by the Sylph Serum, and as a result his desires demand satisfaction, something he attempts to handle without any intentional harm to others. There is no information how successfully he has accomplished this, yet those who have received his attention, report that the aftercare almost made it worth it…almost.

### As a Resistance Member

Thanks to his connection with Hydie, and his own retained empathy Song with some effort has joined the resistance, and works as an active assistant to Hydie, in smuggling operations to get toy's out of the city, or if not possible, care for them when able.

While an oddity even among their numbers, he is not as active in open operations that would risk him being exposed. His life being forfeited if he were to be discovered, as failure would be seen as a failure of the Sylph project as a whole. Due to his status several of the toy's within the organization consider him an unwanted addition to their number not least of which because the larger women seem to enjoy that he doesn't cry as much when they need a little release.

## Preferences

- With a female, he is naturally subservient, despite size, even to a smaller female his training and actions as a toy and prior life see him utterly at their command. While playful he will not directly disobey and order, though he has been known to turn situations as best he can to protect himself and others when possible. He does also harbor a deep wish to still have companionship even in this twisted new world, though that seems less likely by the day.
- With a Toy, he is rather neutral, while observing them as of course living people, some event has caused him to view them as if he would any stranger, with empathy, but not outright trust. He cares for toys and wants what is best for them, yet, he does not assume their place below the girls and him makes them all beacons of purity. However, once getting to know them, he will do everything he can to help those who are worth saving.
- Shrunken Toys, an odd dichotomy is that prior to T-Day his own fantasies revolved around various "Size" related situations, resulting in a unique view of shrunken toys. While his position is mostly the same as with a Toy, his baser desires and status does result in a more dominate and playful personality coming forth more often. While he is not actively harmful, his care for them over what they can do for him is drastically lopsided.

## Usage Guide

- Song does not kill intentionally or in a fit of pleasure if there is any other option. No death directly associated with him would be due to an avoidable choice outside his direct control. He is, however, pragmatic, and if the trolly question were presented, between saving five toys, or one, he would choose the five, while dealing with the guilt of that choice however he could. Ex. He could trip and fall on a toy, but not intentionally stomp them.

- Song has been deployed for several enforcement actions within the wilds and has remained a high performer in "capturing" marks, who are then returned to the pound. Given Hydie's affiliation, it is likely these "captures" are less due to skill and rather a means of resistance movements.
- It is known that he is an adamant supporter of a particular resistance plan "Delphi"
